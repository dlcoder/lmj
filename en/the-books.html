The books

The Lugares Míticos de Jaén books continued and widened the work of making the province's heritage known, alongside the weekly work on the website. We published two, in 2015 and 2017, of sixteen chapters each, one place per chapter: monumental, industrial, natural and wartime heritage. Every chapter gathered more than a dozen photographs and a text written by someone closely tied to the place — not academic writing, but a close, personal look.

Neither book is on sale any more. This page remains as a record of both, and above all of the people who made them possible. The books themselves were published in Spanish.

The first book (2015)

It was funded through a crowdfunding campaign we launched on Verkami in late December 2014. We needed €4,500 to make the project viable and raised almost €6,000 over the 40 days of the campaign, which paid for a first print run of 600 copies. The rest was the work contributed by the authors and the team.

It was presented on 25 June 2015 at the Old Chapel of the Hospital San Juan de Dios in Jaén, alongside the sixteen authors, on the same day the project turned two.

Where it was presented

  • Jaén, 25 June 2015, at the Old Chapel of the Hospital San Juan de Dios.
  • Rus, 17 July 2015, at the Casa de la Juventud.
  • Martos, 17 October 2015, at the Casa Municipal de Cultura Francisco Delicado, with Club Fotográfico Click Click Martos.
  • Baños de la Encina, 6 November 2015, at the Museo del Territorio, with the town's tourist office.
  • IES Auringis, 26 February 2016.
  • Pegalajar, 26 February 2016, with the Asociación de Fuente de la Reja.
  • Úbeda, 19 March 2016, at the Church of San Lorenzo, with Fundación Huerta de San Antonio during the Festival de la Palabra.

The second book (2017)

We went back to Verkami in mid-September 2017. We asked for a minimum of €4,500 and raised around €5,500. As with the first book, the rest was the work contributed by the authors and the team.

Its sixteen chapters open with "Jaén, cerca y lejos", a piece by Antonio Muñoz Molina. The cover is the work of Juan Montoro Peralta, el Creata.

It was presented on 5 December 2017, again at the Old Chapel of the Hospital San Juan de Dios in Jaén.
