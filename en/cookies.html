Cookie policy

This is a static site: it has no server of its own, it stores nothing about you and it sets no cookies by itself.

To know how many visits it gets we use Umami, a statistics tool that sets no cookies and stores no personal data: it counts visits in aggregate and anonymously (which pages are seen, from which country and with which browser), without following you from one visit to the next or across sites.

A few pages do embed third-party content, and those services may set their own cookies when you load the page containing them:

  • Four articles include a YouTube video (Google): Sabiote Castle I, El Castillo de La Iruela, Iglesia de Santo Domingo and Mina-Fundición de la Tortilla. They load in its privacy-enhanced mode, which sets no cookies until you press play.
  • The pages of the first book and the second include their presentations hosted on Speakerdeck.

Those cookies are set by those services, not by us, and are governed by their own policies. If you would rather avoid them, you can block third-party cookies in your browser settings: the rest of the site works exactly the same.
