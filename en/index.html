The books

The project published two books, in 2015 and 2017. The first is out of print; a few copies of the second may still be left in the bookshops in Jaén, Úbeda and Martos that sold it. Both were deposited in local and provincial libraries.

If you are looking for one, write to us at participa@lugaresmiticosdejaen.com.
