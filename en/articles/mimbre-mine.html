Mimbre Mine

Published
29/09/2014
Municipality
Linares
Region
Sierra Morena
Categories
Monuments, Nature
  • The Mimbre mine path starts at the hermitage of the Virgen de Linarejos and passes the San Luis smelter, the Calvario mine, the Mimbre mine and the San Vicente shaft.
  • One of the mining railways of Linares ran along this path, served by the old Linares–Los Salidos railway.
  • The Mimbre mine is one of the oldest in the area, already producing a great deal in the 17th century. Its impressive chimney, 40 metres high, can still be seen today.
  • The San Vicente shaft, 1,008 metres deep, is the deepest in this district. It is especially remembered for the tragic accident in which 6 miners died during the mine’s last haul.
  • May this article be a tribute to them, and to all the lives cut short by the extreme hardship of mining.

More about this place…

The landscape of Linares shows how important mining has been to the city. From the mid-19th century it grew exponentially, its population multiplied and the United Kingdom, France, Germany and Belgium opened consular offices there. Mining ended altogether in 1991; paths such as the one to the Mimbre mine keep alive the memory of an activity that transformed the town.
