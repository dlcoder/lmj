Peña del Águila Flying Centre

Published on 04/12/2017
Mancha Real (Comarca Metropolitana de Jaén)
Nature
  • Opened in June 2016, this free-flight centre makes the province of Jaén, and this area in particular, a landmark for lovers of unpowered flight.
  • To the spectacular landscapes Mancha Real offers as one of the gateways to the Sierra Mágina Natural Park, it adds unbeatable access.
  • Both the take-off and the landing areas can be reached by car, with a return trip to the top of under 10 minutes.
  • Besides unpowered flight, this emblematic area is ideal for other sports such as hiking, running and mountain biking.

More about this place…

On the outskirts of Mancha Real, the Peña del Águila has long been a usual leisure spot both for the people of the town and for visitors from other parts of the province. This natural setting, ideal for hiking or mountain biking, has since June 2016 also been a landmark for lovers of unpowered flight. The new flying centre joins the existing one at Siete Pilillas, in nearby Pegalajar, making the province of Jaén an outstanding place for the sport.
