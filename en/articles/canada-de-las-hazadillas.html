Cañada de las Hazadillas

Published
17/02/2014
Municipality
Jaén
  • The “Cañada de las Hazadillas” is one of the most popular leisure areas for the people of Jaén.
  • It lies in the Monte de la Sierra peri-urban park, barely 20 kilometres from Jaén.
  • It is common to see people from Jaén filling bottles at its natural springs.
  • Its picnic areas, benches and barbecue spots make it an ideal place to spend the day. It also has a nature classroom for 60 people.
  • Many people from Jaén will have enjoyed the old wooden forts as children.

More about this place…

Picnic areas, natural springs, barbecue spots, benches and above all a wonderful natural setting have made the Cañada de las Hazadillas one of the leisure areas the people of Jaén visit most. It lies barely 20 km from the capital, inside the Monte de la Sierra peri-urban park, which covers more than 2,700 hectares. Several generations of Jaén children have grown up playing in the old wooden forts of “la Cañada”, enjoying nature at its purest.
