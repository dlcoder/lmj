Concordia Park

Published on 03/03/2014
Jaén (Comarca Metropolitana de Jaén)
Nature, Monuments
  • Next to Plaza de las Batallas, the former La Victoria park took its current name in 2009 under the Historical Memory Law.
  • The park was conceived in 1927 as part of the city expansion plan drawn up by Luis Berges, although work only began after the Civil War.
  • The park covers 16,489 m² and has four rectangular walks and a central one, closed in a semicircle by a drive beside the gardens.
  • The bicycle track and the duck pond have marked the childhood of many generations of people from Jaén.

More about this place…

Concordia Park stands next to Plaza de las Batallas, in the very centre of Jaén. Luis Berges planned it in the 1927 Expansion Plan, which radically changed the face of the city. Covering almost two hectares, the park is made up of four rectangular walks and a central one, closed in a semicircle by a drive beside the gardens. Well known for its duck pond and its old bicycle track, the former La Victoria park changed its name in 2009 under the Historical Memory Law.
