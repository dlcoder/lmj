El Guindo

  • In the municipality of La Carolina, it lies barely 8 kilometres from the town.
  • This small mining village, which had 800 inhabitants in the 1950s, is laid out along the roads to the El Guindo and La Manzana shafts.
  • Many of its houses keep their original style despite having been restored.
  • After the mines of Linares declined, those of La Carolina became one of the country’s leading producers of lead.
  • El Guindo, also known as Los Guindos, is another witness to how important mining was in the north of the province.

More about this place…

The village of El Guindo lies 8 kilometres from La Carolina, on the road to the mining village of El Centenillo. Still lived in today, its houses keep their original style despite being restored. The village sits next to the El Guindo and La Manzana shafts, two of the most important mines in the whole area.
