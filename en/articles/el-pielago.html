El Piélago

Published on 28/04/2014
Vilches (El Condado)
Nature
  • In Vilches, El Piélago is a spectacular spot that the Junta de Andalucía declared a Natural Monument in 2003.
  • The landscape is made up of great granite boulders over which the waters of the Guarrizas river leap, forming two lovely waterfalls.
  • The Via Augusta ran through it, linking the capital of the Roman Empire with Gades, today’s Cádiz.
  • The Vadollano bridge, built in the 3rd century BC, was part of that road. It was built to cross the drops of El Piélago, making it easier to carry the metal from the mines of Sierra Morena.

More about this place…

El Piélago is a beautiful natural spot in Vilches, and one of the 28 Natural Monuments in all of Andalusia. A spectacular landscape of great granite boulders over which the waters of the Guarrizas river tumble, forming two large waterfalls. Besides its natural value, with an important flora and birdlife, El Piélago has great historical value. The Via Augusta, which linked Rome with Cádiz, ran through it. The Vadollano bridge, built in the 3rd century BC, bears witness to it, and its remains can still be visited.
