La Mella

Published
20/10/2014
Municipality
Jaén
  • Rising to 1,244 metres, the Mella hill is famous for its ridge, or “crest”.
  • This natural wall between the Almodóvar hill and the Morteros hill is one of the emblems of the city of Jaén.
  • It is popularly known as “la mella” for the gap, or notch, in the middle of the ridge.
  • From it we can enjoy splendid views over the whole city and some of the nearby peaks.

More about this place…

Popularly known as “la mella” (the gap), this natural wall is one of the emblems of the city of Jaén. The notch in the middle of its spectacular rocky crest earns it this affectionate nickname from the people of Jaén. Easy to see from almost anywhere in the city, its “crest” offers wonderful views over the whole of it.
