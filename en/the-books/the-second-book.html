The project The books

The second book (2017)

We went back to Verkami in mid-September 2017. We asked for a minimum of €4,500 and raised around €5,500. As with the first book, the rest was the work contributed by the authors and the team.

Its sixteen chapters open with "Jaén, cerca y lejos", a piece by Antonio Muñoz Molina. The cover is the work of Juan Montoro Peralta, el Creata.

It was presented on 5 December 2017, again at the Old Chapel of the Hospital San Juan de Dios in Jaén.

Where to find it

A few copies may still be left in the bookshops that sold it: Metrópolis, Don Libro, Picasso and Delfos in Jaén; Libros Prohibidos and El Agente Secreto Libros in Úbeda; and San Antonio in Martos. Copies were also deposited in local and provincial libraries, where they should still be available: the Andalusian public library network catalogue shows which ones hold it (ISBN 978-84-697-6984-3). If you cannot find one, write to us at participa@lugaresmiticosdejaen.com.
