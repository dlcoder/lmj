The project The books

The first book (2015)

It was funded through a crowdfunding campaign we launched on Verkami in late December 2014. We needed €4,500 to make the project viable and raised almost €6,000 over the 40 days of the campaign, which paid for a first print run of 600 copies. The rest was the work contributed by the authors and the team.

It was presented on 25 June 2015 at the Old Chapel of the Hospital San Juan de Dios in Jaén, alongside the sixteen authors, on the same day the project turned two.

Where to find it

The first book is out of print. Copies were deposited in local and provincial libraries, where they should still be available: the Andalusian public library network catalogue shows which ones hold it (ISBN 978-84-606-8381-0). If you are looking for one, write to us at participa@lugaresmiticosdejaen.com.

Where it was presented

  • Jaén, 25 June 2015, at the Old Chapel of the Hospital San Juan de Dios.
  • Rus, 17 July 2015, at the Casa de la Juventud.
  • Martos, 17 October 2015, at the Casa Municipal de Cultura Francisco Delicado, with Club Fotográfico Click Click Martos.
  • Baños de la Encina, 6 November 2015, at the Museo del Territorio, with the town's tourist office.
  • IES Auringis, 26 February 2016.
  • Pegalajar, 26 February 2016, with the Asociación de Fuente de la Reja.
  • Úbeda, 19 March 2016, at the Church of San Lorenzo, with Fundación Huerta de San Antonio during the Festival de la Palabra.
